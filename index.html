How smart sorting works

01

Product arrives

Items move along the production line and enter the inspection area.

02

Visual inspection

A camera captures each item for an AI-assisted quality check.

03

Quality decision

The system assesses the item and determines whether it meets the set criteria.

04

Automatic sorting

Items are directed onward or separated for review based on the inspection.
